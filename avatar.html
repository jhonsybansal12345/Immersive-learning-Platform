Track 2: Avatar

Recreate a DSA lecture as slides with voice-over, then create an avatar that closely resembles the lecturer. Everything you produce must come from a single source of truth: the chosen lecturer's lectures.

Source of truth (pick one lecturer)

  • Erik Demaine
  • Virginia Vassilevska Williams

What you need to do

  1. Choose a topic. Pick any DSA topic of medium difficulty. Quick Sort, Heap Sort, or anything at that level or above is fine.
  2. Create the content. Prepare material for your chosen topic, based only on the lecturer's lectures.
  3. Recreate the lecture. Turn that content into a slide deck with voice-over, using Google NotebookLM.
  4. Create the avatar. Make an avatar that closely resembles the lecturer you chose, and have it present the lecture.
  5. Use free tools. Any API or tool is allowed, as long as it is free or available off the shelf.
  6. Prepare the deliverables.
    • A slide deck
    • A video animation with voice-over, in a local language (or another language you choose)
    • The avatar presenting the lecture
Deadline: Monday, 1:00 PM
