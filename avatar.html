Track 2: Avatar

Recreate a DSA lecture as slides with voice-over, then create an avatar that closely resembles the lecturer. Everything you produce must come from a single source of truth.

Source of truth

  • Erik Demaine and Srinivas Devadas — youtube video link and associated course website Open the MIT lecture course

What you need to do

  1. Choose a topic. Pick any DSA topic of medium difficulty. Quick Sort, Heap Sort, or anything at that level or above is fine.
  2. Create the content. Acquire the relevant material for your chosen topic.
  3. Recreate the lecture. Turn that content into a slide deck with voice-over, using Google NotebookLM.
  4. Create the avatar. Create a realistic AI avatar that closely resembles the selected lecturer and use it to deliver the lecture in an engaging and professional manner.
  5. Use free tools. Any API or tool is allowed, as long as it is free or available off the shelf.
  6. Prepare the deliverables.
    • The avatar presenting the lecture(short video)
