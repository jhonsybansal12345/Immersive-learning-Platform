Track 1: RAG

Build a RAG system that answers questions from a single source of truth: lecture content on Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA).

Source of truth

  • Erik Demaine and Srinivas Devadas — youtube video link Watch the YouTube Playlist and associated course website Open the MIT lecture course

What you need to do

  1. Choose a topic. Pick any DSA topic of medium difficulty. Quick Sort, Heap Sort, or anything at that level or above is fine.
  2. Create the content. Acquire the relevant material for your chosen topic.
  3. Create the RAG. Build a RAG pipeline that uses only that content as its knowledge base, so every answer is grounded in the source.
  4. Deploy it. Deploy the RAG system through an interactive interface hosted on Hugging Face.
  5. Prepare the deliverables.
    • Develop a RAG system integrated with a comprehensive archive of all study materials provided to it
    • A slide deck & video animation with voice-over(voice-over should be in a local language)
